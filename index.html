About

I am a PhD researcher in Design Engineering at Imperial College London, supervised by Professor Pierre Pinson. My work lies at the intersection of machine learning, data markets, optimisation, and mechanism design.

I am particularly interested in active learning markets, cost-aware data acquisition, multi-agent learning, bandit algorithms, Thompson Sampling, and forecasting under uncertainty.

Research

Cost-Aware Active Learning Markets

Designing market mechanisms and learning algorithms for acquiring labels and data when observations are costly.

Online and Sequential Data Acquisition

Studying how learning agents make acquisition decisions over time under changing data distributions and limited budgets.

Multi-Agent Data Markets

Developing allocation and learning methods for markets with multiple buyers, sellers, and strategic constraints.
